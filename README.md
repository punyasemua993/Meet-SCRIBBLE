# Meet-SCRIBBLE
Meet SCRIBBLE What is it? A living anxiety doodle. That feeling when you had 3 coffees, 2 hours of sleep, and 17 tabs open — but you're still vibing.
Core Design Logic (why it won't get disqualified):

- 100% Original Silhouette: Round scribble-fur blob + 1 giant bulging eye + 1 squinted eye + long noodle arms. You can recognize it from just the outline.
- Not Pepe / Not Doge: No frog, no shiba, no hat trick. It's a new species.
- No AI Errors: Clean hands, consistent feet, correct anatomy — I built the sheet with 5 expressions to prove it holds up from any angle.
  
**Why it's Relatable & Meme-able:**

1. The Eye: That one huge, over-caffeinated eye is pure "me at 2am" energy. Instant reaction meme.
2. The Coffee: The spilling coffee is never stable — it's the chaos prop. "Me trying to hold my life together" = Scribble holding coffee.
3. Expressions for days: Top-left = pure hype, Top-right = panic, Bottom-left = annoyed / "bruh", Bottom-right = smug chaos, Center = unhinged joy. You can caption all 5 without editing anything.

Name is SCRIBBLE. Tagline: "Embraces the mess.
